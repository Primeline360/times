01 • Vision & value proposition

Wear the memory. Fund the future.

Vision: to grow a recognisable Namibian circular-fashion platform where fashion, remembrance and cancer awareness create both commercial and community value.

01

Fashion with meaning

Customers do not only buy an item; they support a story of remembrance, survival and hope.

02

Waste becomes value

Upcycled, organic and thrifted materials reduce waste while creating distinctive small-batch fashion pieces.

03

Impact built into the model

Fashion shows, donations, sales and partnerships create a platform to raise funds and awareness for cancer-related community support.

02 • How it works

One garment. Four layers of value.

The runway launches the story, while the brand continues through product sales, partnerships, community activations and digital engagement.

STEP 01

Source

Collect usable offcuts, thrifted textiles, surplus fabrics and responsibly sourced materials.

STEP 02

Create

Design pink garments and accessories that carry a remembrance or survivor story.

STEP 03

Show & sell

Connect with audiences through fashion shows, pop-ups, digital channels and the online collection.

STEP 04

Give & grow

Raise awareness, support agreed cancer-related initiatives and reinvest commercial income to grow responsibly.

03 • Our market

Three audiences. One shared cause.

Pink Threads connects the people who buy, the organisations that support, and the communities that benefit.

CUSTOMERS

Conscious fashion buyers

Students, young professionals, fashion-conscious consumers, supporters of local design and buyers who value products with a social story.

AUDIENCE & FUNDERS

Community + sponsors

Community audiences, companies, retailers, media, influencers, CSR/ESG teams and people personally affected by cancer.

BENEFICIARIES

People and communities

Cancer survivors, affected families and selected community initiatives. The Cancer Association of Namibia is a potential beneficiary or partner, subject to formal agreement.

04 • Revenue, funding & business model

Beyond one fashion show.

The runway is a launch platform—not the whole business. Pink Threads can continue through recurring product sales, events, collaborations and a growing digital collection.

Product salesAccessories, garments and limited collections.
Event ticketsFuture fashion shows and themed activations.
SponsorshipCorporate support for collections, events or impact campaigns.
DonationsVoluntary fundraising during shows, events and digital campaigns.
CollaborationsBranded collections, retailer partnerships and custom campaigns.
BUSINESS MODEL

Create → Sell → Reinvest

Earn commercial income from products and collaborations, cover operating costs, reinvest in new collections and allocate agreed fundraising proceeds transparently.

DIGITAL MODEL

Website + online collection

Use the website to tell product stories, show available pieces, receive enquiries, promote events and later support online ordering.

INNOVATION

Phygital fashion

Combine physical fashion experiences with QR-linked stories, social content, online product viewing, digital donations and future e-commerce.

RISK

Greenwashing

Control: publish real material and impact figures; do not claim achievements before they occur.

RISK

Donation trust

Control: separate donation records, receipts and agreed beneficiary reporting.

RISK

Supply & quality

Control: multiple material sources, quality checks and small-batch production.

RISK

Low demand

Control: customer testing, pre-orders, social feedback and limited runs.

RISK

Sensitive stories

Control: informed consent and respectful survivor/family storytelling.

RISK

Event delivery

Control: clear responsibilities, timelines and backup venue/technical plans.
