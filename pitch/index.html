What we stand for

Purpose in every piece.

Pink Threads brings together sustainable design, remembrance and community action. Our pieces are intended to spark conversation, reduce waste and create a platform for meaningful support.

REMEMBER

Stories that matter

Fashion becomes a respectful way to honour those lost, celebrate survivors and keep important conversations visible.

REIMAGINE

Waste into value

Recovered, thrifted and responsibly sourced materials are transformed into distinctive garments and accessories.

REINVEST

Impact that grows

Sales, events, donations and partnerships can support awareness, community initiatives and future fair-work opportunities.

The collection

Made to be worn. Made to be remembered.

Our collection uses pink as a symbol of strength, remembrance and hope, while demonstrating how ordinary materials can be reimagined through design.
