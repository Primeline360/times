01 • The opportunity

Fashion can carry memory, meaning and impact.

Pink Threads of Remembrance treats discarded materials as a design resource, connecting circular fashion, creativity and a social purpose that can grow responsibly in Namibia.

01

Recover value

Redirect useful materials from waste streams and give them a second life through design.

02

Create desirable products

Turn recovered material into pieces people want to wear, gift and talk about.

03

Build local impact

Use growth to create fair-work opportunities and support practical community initiatives in Namibia.

02 • How it works

From discarded material to statement piece.

A simple circular model: source responsibly, redesign creatively, make with care and reinvest part of the value into people and community.

STEP 01

Source

Identify usable discarded, surplus or overlooked materials.

STEP 02

Reimagine

Develop concepts that celebrate texture, story and imperfection.

STEP 03

Create

Produce small-batch pieces with a focus on quality and uniqueness.

STEP 04

Reinvest

Grow fair-work opportunities and support selected community projects.
